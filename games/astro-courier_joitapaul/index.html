COURIER BRIEFING

A delivery run above the clouds

Collect every parcel on the route, avoid the moving hazard drones, then reach the glowing drop gate.

  • Move: A / D or the left and right arrow keys.
  • Jump: Press W, the up arrow, or Space while on a platform. Tap again to jump again.
  • Goal: Collect every parcel before entering the delivery gate.
  • Scoring: Parcels and completed deliveries add points. Avoid hazards to keep your lives.
  • Pause / restart: P or Escape pauses; R restarts the current district.
