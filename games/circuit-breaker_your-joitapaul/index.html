HOW TO PLAY

Restore the signal

Rotate the circuit tiles until one continuous connection runs from the power source to the target. Each board grows larger and adds more turns.

  • Rotate: Click or tap a tile, or focus it and press Enter or Space. Every turn rotates it clockwise by 90 degrees.
  • Navigate: Use the arrow keys to move between rotatable tiles. Energized wires show your current connection.
  • Score: Complete a board for 500 points minus 10 per move, with a 100-point minimum.
  • Progress: Finish each board to unlock the next. Restart with R or the reset button.
  • Pause: Press P or Escape to pause, then resume when you are ready.
