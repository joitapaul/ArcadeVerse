DIVE BRIEFING

A current is pulling you in

Swim east through the reef. Air bubbles refill your tank; touching a moving reef mine costs oxygen.

  • Swim: W / A / S / D or the arrow keys.
  • Collect: Swim close to an air bubble to refill oxygen and earn points.
  • Survive: Reach the exit before your oxygen reaches zero.
  • Pause / restart: P or Escape pauses; R restarts the dive.
